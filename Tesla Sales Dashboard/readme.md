# Leveraging Dashboard Design for Business KPI Tracking: Insights into Tesla’s Sales Records

## **1.0 Executive Summary**

This project explores a 1000-synthetic Tesla sales dataset covering 2023 - 2025, to uncover patterns in revenue, sale unit, top-demand countries, customer types, amongst others. An interactive dashboard is designed using Microsoft Excel to track and visualize this information. The result shows that over the 3-year period, a total of $262,940,000 has been generated for 2,972 Tesla vehicle sales. Also, the USA is the largest Tesla vehicle demand-country (by revenue), and the Model X is the largest unit-selling vehicle model. 

The result shows that dashboard design with Microsoft Excel can effectively track business KPIs. Like Tesla, insights of dashboard design supports strategic planning, performance evaluation, revenue optimization, and long-term growth.

## **2.0 Business Problem/Challenge**

Tracking business key performance indicators (KPIs) such as revenue, sale quantities, product-demand locations and customer stratifications, is crucial for a robust growth. Therefore, this project develops an interactive dashboard using a Tesla sales transaction dataset with Microsoft Excel, to show how businesses can track them.

## **3.0 Methodology**

A synthetic Tesla sales transaction dataset comprising 1000 customer entries and capturing 14 columns is used in this project. Key features of the dataset include customer id, transaction date, sale amount, sale unit, country of order, and customer type. Below are additional key descriptions and actions.

**Data Cleaning:** Checked for missing and null values

**Dashboard Design:** Built pivot tables for dynamic aggregation; designed interactive charts and KPIs; added slicers to filter by year, car model, and country.

**Tools:** Microsoft Excel, Pivot Table, Slicers, Connection-Reporting, Formula Writing

**Skills:** Microsoft Excel Data Analysis & Visualization, Pivot Table Development, Interactive Dashboard Design, Slicer Development, Business Insight & Development, Chart Design, Business Reporting & Storytelling

## **4.0 Key Findings & Business Recommendations**

**-	Revenue and Sale Units:** A total of 2,972 Tesla vehicles were sold between 2023 and 2025, generating a revenue of $262,940,000. During this period, 2024 recorded the highest revenue ($130,216,000) with 1,463 unit sales, and followed by 2023 which generated $87,725,000 selling 997 vehicle units. 

**-	Top-Demand countries:** The USA is the largest Tesla vehicle-demand country globally ($30,560,000), followed by the UK ($30,222,000) and France ($29,816,000). The result shows that prioritizing these countries, especially the USA, in terms of customer purchasing incentives and revenue-stimulating initiatives will grow margins. Also, strengthening investment and awareness at low-revenue performing countries, while expanding to new ones, will accelerate overall business growth.

**-	Highest-Demand Vehicle Model:** The Tesla Model X records the highest unit sales among all the vehicle models. The Model X sold 755 units, accounting for 25.4% of all unit-sales. This is followed by the Cybertruck (25.2%) and the Model 3 (24.87%). Leveraging this insight to promote Model X across all target-locations, especially in the USA which is the highest Model X unit-purchasing country, with strategic customer incentives, will increase revenue margins.

**-	Variation in Revenue per Customer Groups:** Transactions with corporate bodies generated the highest revenue ($138,905,000), compared to individuals ($124,033,000). Based on this result, designing promotional packages and advertisements targeting corporate bodies, while forging innovative marketing solutions to grow transactions with individuals, will boost long-term business revenue.
